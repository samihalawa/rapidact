## Where Article 50 hits digital agencies

| AI you probably use | What the law wants |
|---|---|
| Chat widgets you installed for clients | The deployer duty is the client's, but the blame is yours — document disclosure on every build. |
| AI-generated ad creatives and influencers | Synthetic campaign media needs visible labels, including when clients repost it. |
| AI-written client content | Bulk AI blog and landing copy needs labels or documented human editorial review. |
