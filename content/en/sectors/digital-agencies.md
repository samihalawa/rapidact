# EU AI Act for Digital agencies: what you must disclose by 2 August 2026

Your clients' chatbots and AI creatives put disclosure duties on their sites — but agencies get the call when it goes wrong. How to productize Article 50 for your roster. This is the plain-English playbook — what triggers the law in digital agencies, what to fix first, and how to do it without consultants.

## Where Article 50 hits digital agencies

| AI you probably use | What the law wants |
|---|---|
| Chat widgets you installed for clients | The deployer duty is the client's, but the blame is yours — document disclosure on every build. |
| AI-generated ad creatives and influencers | Synthetic campaign media needs visible labels, including when clients repost it. |
| AI-written client content | Bulk AI blog and landing copy needs labels or documented human editorial review. |

## The order of operations

1. **Scan everything public** — your site, booking flow, and any client-facing portal. [Free scan →](/scanner)
2. **Establish the roles** — identify the provider, deployer and exact Article 50 trigger for each system.
3. **Implement the right notice** — use the [free one-script badge](/article-50#install) for a direct-interaction disclosure where it fits.
4. **Treat content separately** — distinguish provider machine-readable marking from deployer duties for deepfakes and public-interest text.
5. **Keep proportionate proof** — record live URLs, wording, owners and dated desktop/mobile checks without tracking visitors.
