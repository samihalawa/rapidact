# EU AI Act for Insurance & financial services: what you must disclose by 2 August 2026

insurers and fintechs are early AI adopters — and highly visible Article 50 targets. This is the plain-English playbook — what triggers the law in insurance & financial services, what to fix first, and how to do it without consultants.

## Where Article 50 hits insurance & financial services

| AI you probably use | What the law wants |
|---|---|
| Quote and claims chatbots | Conversational quote flows and claims assistants — disclosure at first contact. |
| AI voice agents on phone lines | Natural-sounding voice AI handling calls must identify itself — we already see insurers where customers thank the 'person' by name. |
| AI-written advice content | Public guides and comparisons drafted by AI — label or document editorial review. |

## The order of operations

1. **Scan everything public** — your site, booking flow, and any client-facing portal. [Free scan →](/scanner)
2. **Establish the roles** — identify the provider, deployer and exact Article 50 trigger for each system.

5. **Keep proportionate proof** — record live URLs, wording, owners and dated desktop/mobile checks without tracking visitors.
