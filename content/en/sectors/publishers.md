# EU AI Act for Publishers & media: what you must disclose by 2 August 2026

publishers sit at the center of Article 50(4). What the editorial-review exemption really requires. This is the plain-English playbook — what triggers the law in publishers & media, what to fix first, and how to do it without consultants.

## Where Article 50 hits publishers & media

| AI you probably use | What the law wants |
|---|---|
| AI-drafted public-interest articles | Label required unless a human editor substantively reviewed, edited and takes responsibility. |
| AI-generated images and video | Realistic synthetic visuals must be labeled visibly. |
| AI moderation and reader-facing bots | Comment assistants and reader chatbots are AI interactions — disclose them. |

## Sector reality check

The 'a human checked it' exemption is the most abused sentence in the Act. Without a documented review trail — who reviewed, what changed, when — the exemption doesn't exist.
