# EU AI Act fines: how much can Article 50 violations cost?

**Up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher. Article 50 transparency violations sit in the Act's mid-tier penalty band under Article 99, with lower caps for SMEs.**

## The penalty tiers, context included

The AI Act has three fine bands:

| Violation | Maximum fine |
|---|---|
| Prohibited AI practices (Art. 5) | €35M or 7% of turnover |
| Most high-risk and GPAI obligations | €15M or 3% of turnover |
| **Transparency (Art. 50)** | **€15M or 3% of turnover** |
| Supplying misleading information to authorities | €7.5M or 1% of turnover |

## What "whichever is higher" means in practice

For a €50M-turnover company, 3% is €1.5M. For a €1B company, €30M — above the €15M cap, so the cap binds... no wait, "whichever is higher" means the 3% applies when it's bigger. For small companies the percentage is tiny, and national authorities set proportional amounts — but even a scaled-down five-figure fine comes with something worse: a public enforcement notice.
