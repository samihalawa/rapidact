# Can I put ChatGPT (or any custom GPT) on my website in the EU?

**Yes — but from 2 August 2026 you must tell visitors they're interacting with AI, clearly and at first contact. There's no ban on embedded AI assistants; there's a transparency duty on whoever deploys them.**

## Allowed, with a label

Nothing in the AI Act stops you embedding a custom GPT, a Botpress agent, or a homegrown LLM assistant on your site. The law regulates honesty about it, not existence of it.

## Your three duties as the deployer

1. **Disclosure** — visitors must know it's AI before chatting. [How →](/requirements/chatbot-ai-disclosure)
2. **Content labels** — if it generates public-facing content (images, articles), label those too.
3. **Data honesty** — GDPR still applies to what the bot collects. Different law, same bot. [AI Act vs GDPR →](/answers/ai-act-vs-gdpr)

## The custom-build trap

Vendor bots at least have docs. A custom GPT embed has *nothing* — no default label, no vendor duty to lean on. The disclosure is 100% yours. Free fix: [one script tag →](/platforms/custom-website)
