# Who enforces the EU AI Act — and how will they find me?

**National market-surveillance authorities in each member state enforce Article 50, coordinated by the EU AI Office. They can scan public websites exactly like anyone else — your chatbot and your content are visible evidence.**

## The enforcement machinery

- **AI Office (European Commission)** — coordinates, handles GPAI.
- **National market surveillance authorities** — the boots on the ground for Article 50 in each country.
- **National courts** — where complaints and damages end up.

## How violations surface

1. **Automated scanning** — checking public sites for undisclosed AI is trivially automatable (we do it free; so can they).
2. **Competitor reports** — the cheapest corporate espionage: reporting your rival's unlabeled AI.
3. **Consumer complaints** — "their bot pretended to be human" is a one-paragraph complaint.
4. **Media investigations** — journalists love a named-and-shamed AI story.
