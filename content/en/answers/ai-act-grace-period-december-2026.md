# The December 2, 2026 grace period: what it covers (and what it doesn't)

**Only one thing: machine-readable marking of AI-generated content for generative AI systems already on the market before 2 August 2026. Chatbot disclosure, deepfake labels and deployer duties have NO grace period — they apply from 2 August 2026.**

## One grace period, precisely scoped

Article 111's transitional provisions give providers of generative AI systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026 until **2 December 2026** to implement the technical, machine-readable marking of Article 50(2).

## What people wrongly assume it covers

- Chatbot disclosure → **no grace period**. Live from 2 August 2026.
- Deepfake labeling → **no grace period**.
- Deployer duties → **no grace period**.

The grace period is a technical-implementation window for AI *providers'* marking tech — not a business delay for websites. If your chatbot is live in August, your disclosure must be too. [Fix it before August →](/scanner)
