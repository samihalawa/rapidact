# The Digital Omnibus delay: what actually changed (the two-clock trap)

**The Digital Omnibus (agreed May 2026) delayed high-risk AI Act obligations to December 2027 and August 2028 — but explicitly left Article 50 transparency rules on schedule for 2 August 2026. Reading 'AI Act delayed' as 'everything delayed' is the most common compliance mistake of 2026.**

## Why everyone is confused

The Digital Omnibus package was sold as simplification and delay — and it did delay the heavy machinery: Annex III high-risk obligations moved to 2 December 2027, Annex I to 2 August 2028. Headlines said "AI Act delayed".

## What it did NOT touch

- **Article 50 transparency duties** — still 2 August 2026.
- GPAI obligations — already live since August 2025.
- The December 2026 marking grace period mechanics.

## Why this is the trap

The transparency rules are the ones that apply to ordinary websites — your chatbot, your AI images, your AI text. The delayed rules mostly concern AI in recruitment, credit, critical infrastructure — things most marketing teams don't run.
