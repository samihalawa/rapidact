# Is there an EU AI Act exemption for small businesses?

**No. Article 50 transparency obligations apply by use case, not company size — a five-person shop with an AI chatbot has the same disclosure duty as an enterprise. Only the fine amounts consider scale.**

## Where the confusion comes from

Parts of the AI Act include SME-friendly measures (support programs, regulatory sandboxes, proportional fines). People read "SME support" and hear "SME exemption". There isn't one — not for transparency.

## What actually applies to a small business

- **Chatbot disclosure** — yes, even with one widget and three employees.
- **AI content labeling** — yes, even for a two-person marketing team.
- **Deepfake labeling** — yes.
- **Fines** — scaled down for SMEs, but they start from "painful", not "symbolic".

## The good news for small teams

The obligations were designed to be technically simple: a visible notice, labels on synthetic content, records. No audits, no certifications, no conformity assessments — that's the high-risk rulebook (which mostly doesn't apply to marketing AI).
