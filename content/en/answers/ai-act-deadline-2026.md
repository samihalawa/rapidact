# EU AI Act deadlines 2026–2028: the dates that matter

**Article 50 transparency rules apply from 2 August 2026 — on schedule. High-risk rules were delayed to December 2027 / August 2028 by the Digital Omnibus. GPAI rules have applied since August 2025.**

## The full timeline, clean

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 2 Aug 2025 | GPAI obligations began; governance provisions live |
| **2 Aug 2026** | **Article 50 transparency duties apply — chatbots, deepfakes, AI text, emotion AI** |
| 2 Dec 2026 | Grace period ends: machine-readable marking for generative systems already on the market before August |
| 2 Dec 2027 | High-risk Annex III obligations (delayed by the Digital Omnibus) |
| 2 Aug 2028 | High-risk Annex I obligations (delayed) |

## The trap

"The AI Act was delayed" is only true for high-risk rules. The transparency clock never moved — and it's the one that applies to normal business websites. [Why the two-clock confusion is dangerous →](/answers/digital-omnibus-ai-act-delay)
