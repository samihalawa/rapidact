# AI answering phone calls: does Article 50 cover voice AI?

**Yes. Article 50(1) covers any direct interaction between a person and an AI system — including natural-sounding voice AI on your support or sales line. Callers must be told they're speaking with AI.**

## Voice is just chat with audio

The article targets "direct interaction" with AI systems. A voice agent answering calls, qualifying leads, or handling bookings is interacting with humans exactly like a chat widget does — arguably more deceptively, since a good voice is harder to spot than a chat bubble.

## What we've seen in the wild

Spanish insurtechs already run AI voice (built on platforms like ElevenLabs) handling a large share of inbound calls — with customers leaving reviews thanking the "agent" by name. That is precisely the scenario the article prohibits without disclosure.

## The fix for voice

- An upfront line in the call flow: "You're speaking with an automated AI assistant."
- Documented in your IVR/scripts, with an easy route to a human.
