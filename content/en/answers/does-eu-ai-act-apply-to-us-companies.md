# Does the EU AI Act apply to US (non-EU) companies?

**Yes, if their AI systems or AI-generated content reach people in the EU. Article 50 follows the audience, not the headquarters — a US company whose chatbot serves EU visitors must disclose the AI exactly like an EU company.**

## The extraterritorial design

The AI Act copies the GDPR's playbook: obligations attach when AI output is **used in the Union**. Your Delaware C-corp status is irrelevant if a customer in Madrid chats with your bot.

## In practice for non-EU companies

- US/UK/LatAm SaaS with EU users: in scope.
- US brands selling to EU customers via chatbot: in scope.
- US companies with zero EU users: out of scope (for now — several US states are drafting similar transparency laws).
