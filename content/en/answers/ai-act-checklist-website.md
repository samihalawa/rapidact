# EU AI Act website checklist: 8 things to do before 2 August 2026

**Inventory systems, establish provider and deployer roles, implement the relevant interaction or content disclosures, keep proportionate evidence, and set a review date.**

## The checklist

1. **Inventory** — scan your site for AI touchpoints: chat widgets, AI search, embedded assistants. [Free scan →](/scanner)
2. **Role map** — record who is the provider and deployer for each system.
3. **Interaction disclosure** — where Article 50(1) applies, make the AI notice clear by first contact.
4. **Text policy** — label AI-drafted public content, or implement a documented human-review step. [What qualifies →](/answers/human-review-exemption-ai-text)
5. **Voice check** — if AI answers your phone lines, callers must be told.
6. **Vendor clauses** — agencies and AI tool contracts should assign transparency duties explicitly.
7. **Implementation record** — retain live URLs, wording, owners and dated rendered checks. [Why it matters →](/requirements/ai-disclosure-evidence)
8. **Review date** — guidelines and enforcement evolve; recheck quarterly.
