# Deepfake (AI Act definition)

**AI-generated or manipulated audio, image or video content that resembles real persons, objects, places or events and would falsely appear authentic or truthful (Art. 3(60)). Includes AI models, voice clones and face swaps used in marketing.**

> Not just celebrity scams — your AI campaign model counts. Visible label required under Art. 50(4).
