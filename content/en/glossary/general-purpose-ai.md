# General-purpose AI (GPAI)

**AI models trained on broad data that can perform many different tasks — ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and friends. GPAI providers have had their own transparency and documentation duties since August 2025.**

> GPAI rules govern the model makers. Article 50 governs everyone who puts AI in front of people.
