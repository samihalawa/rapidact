# Emotion recognition system

**AI that infers emotions or intentions from biometric data (face, voice, posture). Using it on people requires informing them under Art. 50(3) — and it's banned outright in workplaces and schools under Art. 5.**

> Retail analytics that 'reads shopper mood' is the typical business case. Disclosure is the floor; some contexts are prohibited entirely.
