# High-risk AI system

**AI used in sensitive areas listed in Annex III — recruitment, credit scoring, critical infrastructure, education, law enforcement and similar. These systems face heavy duties (risk management, conformity assessment), delayed to 2027/2028.**

> Your marketing chatbot is NOT high-risk. That's why the 2027 delay doesn't help you — Article 50 is the separate transparency track that starts 2 August 2026.
