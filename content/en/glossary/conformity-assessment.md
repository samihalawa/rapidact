# Conformity assessment

**The formal evaluation high-risk AI systems must pass to prove they meet AI Act requirements — documentation, testing, sometimes a notified body. NOT required for Article 50 transparency duties on normal websites.**

> If someone sells you a conformity assessment for your chatbot, they're selling the high-risk rulebook to the wrong customer. Transparency = disclose and document, no certification.
