# Provider (AI Act)

**The company that develops an AI system or has it developed and places it on the market under its own name — e.g., the chatbot vendor. Providers carry duties like machine-readable marking of AI outputs.**

> Providers mark the outputs; deployers disclose the interaction. Different layers of the same honesty.
