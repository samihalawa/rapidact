# The Code of Practice on AI transparency: what signing (or not) means for you

*2026-07-25 · RapidAct team*

In June 2026 the European Commission published the final Code of Practice on Transparency of AI-Generated Content — the voluntary companion to Article 50, complete with a standard EU "AI" icon set and two sections of measures: one for providers, one for deployers.

## What the Code is (and isn't)

- **Voluntary.** Signing signals good faith and gives a presumption-style defense posture; not signing is not a violation.
- **Practical.** It translates "disclose clearly" into concrete patterns: where labels go, what icons to use, how to word notices.
- **Provider/deployer split.** Model and platform makers have technical marking duties; deployers (you) have visible-disclosure duties.

## What a small website should take from it

1. Use the **standard EU AI icons** rather than inventing your own label design — instant recognizability, zero design work.
2. Follow its **wording patterns** for chat disclosure ("You are interacting with an AI system…").
3. Keep **process evidence** — the Code emphasizes demonstrable measures, which is exactly what an evidence log provides.
