# What the first Article 50 enforcement actions will look like

*2026-07-25 · RapidAct team*

Regulators don't start with random audits. First waves of enforcement under any new EU rulebook follow a predictable logic: maximum educational effect per case.

## The first targets will be

1. **Famous and obvious** — brands whose undisclosed AI is visible to anyone with a browser. Not because they're the worst, but because they teach the market fastest.
2. **Deceptive, not just sloppy** — bots with human names and photos, AI influencers presented as real people, "virtual assistants" that deny being AI. Deception aggravates; honest incompleteness mitigates.
3. **Complaint-driven** — a single well-written consumer or competitor complaint is a cheap case to open.

## The signals to watch

- National market-surveillance authorities publishing Article 50 guidance in local language (Spain's AESIA is among the most active).
- The AI Office's first public statements naming transparency as a priority.
- Trade press covering the first formal information requests — those precede fines by months.
