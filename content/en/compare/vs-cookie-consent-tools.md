# AI disclosure plugins vs cookie-consent tools

**Cookie banners were the GDPR's install-and-forget category; AI disclosure is the AI Act's equivalent. The pattern is identical: regulation creates duty, plugin implements it. But Article 50 needs more than a banner — detection, per-content labels, and evidence.**

| | Cookie consent (GDPR) | AI disclosure (AI Act) |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger | Data collection | AI interaction/content |
| UI | One banner | Per-widget notice + per-content labels |
| Proof | Consent records | Disclosure evidence log |
| Detection needed | Cookies/trackers | 52+ AI platforms |

## The honest verdict

If you learned the cookie-banner playbook in 2018, you already understand this market. The difference: AI disclosure must find what's on your site first — that's why the scanner leads.
